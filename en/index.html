Runs the way you write.

PopCorn is a tiny programming language where values flow left to right through >>. Read it once, and you know what happens.

Try it in your browser Play with it
  • No install
  • Made for beginners
  • Tests built in

02ReadingHow to play: Tap

Read order
is run order.

“Pick the even numbers, double them, add them up.” Compare two ways to write it.

Nested calls (Python)

Start at the innermost nums and work outward. Your eyes jump right to left and back.

PopCorn (in order)

Left to right, in the order you wrote it. Your eyes just move forward.
